## DQ-03 Semantics of the published case_age_days

**Grade:** FAIL

**Question:** Does case_age_days mean what the official dictionary says it means?

**Finding:** The field carries two different meanings. For terminal-status records it matches (date_closed - date_requested) in 99.88% of cases, as documented. For active records — which have no close date at all — it instead matches (extract date - date_requested) in 99.63% of cases. The dictionary describes only the first meaning.

**Treatment:** The project does not use the published field for analysis. active_age_days and lifecycle_days are computed from the dates in 01_clean_base.sql. The published field is retained as case_age_days_published for this comparison only.

## DQ-08 Service taxonomy stability

**Grade:** FAIL

**Finding:** Two problems. Service-level year-over-year rows carry a taxonomy_flag and flagged rows are excluded from every written finding. Wherever a record type is shown against a service name it is the modal value, computed with MODE() rather than picked arbitrarily.

## DQ-09 Duplicate parent/child lineage

**Grade:** WARN

**Question:** Do duplicate children resolve to a parent that exists in scope?

**Finding:** 94,687 case records are duplicate children (13.24%). 3,421 (3.61%) point to a parent outside the three extracts in scope — expected, since a parent closed before 2025 is not in scope. 428 children point at another child.

**Treatment:** issue_key = COALESCE(parent_request_id, service_request_id) collapses one level. An orphaned child keeps its parent's id as its issue_key, so it is still not double-counted against a parent that is present.

## What this dataset cannot tell us

Recorded directly from the City's own published caveat, because it bounds every
conclusion in this project:

> This data includes every user-submitted report and should not be considered an
> official record of City maintenance work. This data does not include details about
> any work performed to fix a problem or the date and time work was completed.

Consequently this project does not, and cannot, measure:

- whether a physical repair happened, or when;
- how long any physical work took — only how long a case record stayed open;
- crew productivity, staffing levels, cost, or budget;
- the true incidence of a problem, only the rate at which it was reported;
- why any pattern exists. Nothing here establishes cause.
